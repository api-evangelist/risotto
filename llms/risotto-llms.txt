# Risotto

> Risotto is an AI help desk tool that automates up to 60% of internal tickets with AI that understands context, not just keywords. It handles knowledge-based questions, software access requests, password resets, and multi-step troubleshooting without employees leaving their existing tools. Smart routing, instant answers, and assistance that actually helps. Risotto integrates with Slack, Jira, Freshservice, Zendesk, Notion, Confluence, and more. Deploys in hours, not weeks.
